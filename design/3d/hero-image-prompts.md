## Today → ReferenceCrystalHero

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium iPhone app hero artwork, no interface. Input image: the supplied three-phone board is a strict visual reference; focus only on the translucent crystal and the pool of light under it in the CENTER phone. Create one isolated, highly photoreal studio render of that same irregular clear-and-amber crystal. Its shape is asymmetrical, broad and low, with many sharp irregular facets; the left/lower interior has rich honey amber absorption and a luminous warm core, while the upper/right exterior has icy clear-blue refraction and bright white specular edges. It rests on a seamless ivory surface (#F7F5F0) with a soft contact shadow and elegant warm caustic rays fanning across the ground. Square 1:1 composition; crystal fills about 65% of image width; gentle empty margin around all sides so it can be cropped for a mobile hero. Match the reference's sophisticated editorial jewelry photography feel. Absolutely no phone, UI, lettering, icons, people, extra objects, border, or watermark.
