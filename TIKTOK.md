# Clearing — TikTok / Build-in-Public Scripts

The research was blunt about one thing: the app is the content. The moat isn't the code, it's distribution. These scripts are built so the phone screen does the selling and you barely have to talk.

Three angles, in priority order. Post the first one first.

---

## Angle 1 — "Dreams came back" (the cannabis-specific hook)

This is the one no quit-app markets and every ex-smoker knows. Vivid dreams returning around day 7 is weird, real, and instantly relatable. Lead with it.

**Hook (first 2 seconds, talking to camera or text-on-screen):**
"Nobody warns you that when you quit weed, your dreams come back. Like, full HD."

**Shot list:**
1. Hook line over a black screen or your face. 2 sec.
2. Cut to the phone: open Clearing, Day 7 on the hero, the gradient warmer than Day 1.
3. Tap into the recovery timeline. Linger on the "1 Week" row: "REM sleep is rebounding. Your dreams are coming back, often surprisingly vivid."
4. Cut back: "Mine were insane the first week. That's how I knew it was working."
5. End on the money saved number ticking up. "Also I've saved $180 not buying."

**On-screen captions:** "day 7 of no weed", "the dreams are unreal", "$180 saved"
**Caption:** the dreams thing is real and nobody talks about it. day 7 hits different.
**Audio:** a calm/ambient trending sound, not hype. The vibe is relief, not grind.
**Hashtags:** #tbreak #quitweed #sobercurious #weedtok #dopaminedetox

## Angle 2 — "Day 1 vs Day 30" (the format that already goes viral)

The "how many days clean" format is the most-shared streak content on the app. Slot straight into it. This one is pure screen recording, zero face needed.

**Hook:** "Day 1 vs Day 30 of quitting." (text on screen, no voiceover needed)

**Shot list:**
1. Screen-record Day 1: hero at 0, cool foggy gradient, money at $0.
2. Hard cut to Day 30: the number big, the gradient now warm gold, $257 saved, 120 sessions skipped.
3. Trigger the 1-month milestone: full-screen confetti, the badge, the haptic. Let it play.
4. Tap "Share my badge". Show the badge card generating.
5. End card: "Clearing. on the app store."

**On-screen captions:** "day 1", "day 30", "$257 i didn't burn"
**Caption:** the confetti when you hit a month got me lol
**Audio:** a build-up/drop sound timed so the drop lands on the confetti.
**Hashtags:** #30daysclean #quitweed #glowup #tbreak #sober

## Angle 3 — "I built this because the other apps were ugly" (build-in-public)

"I built an app" is its own proven format, and the honest version converts: you quit, the existing apps looked like 2014, so you made the one you wanted. Show the craft.

**Hook:** "Every quit-weed app looks like a parking ticket. So I built a nicer one."

**Shot list:**
1. Hook over a quick blink of a generic, ugly competitor screenshot (or a stand-in).
2. Cut to Clearing's onboarding: the breathing leaf, "your tolerance break, beautifully tracked."
3. The projection screen: "$3,120 back, dreams return day 7, tolerance resets day 90."
4. The hero with the gradient that warms as the streak grows. Slow pan.
5. The urge button: the breathing orb expanding. "And when you get a craving, you breathe through it instead of getting shamed."
6. End: "60 days clean. download in bio."

**On-screen captions:** "the other apps:" / "mine:" / "60 days clean"
**Caption:** quit in march, hated every app, so i made clearing. it's free to try.
**Audio:** something clean and a little proud. Not corporate.
**Hashtags:** #buildinpublic #indiedev #quitweed #appstore #solofounder

## Posting notes

- Post Angle 1 first. It's the most specific and least like everything else in the niche.
- One video is a coin flip. Ten videos is a strategy. Re-shoot the same three angles weekly with different first lines until one hits, then make ten more of that one.
- The hero screen and the milestone confetti are the two shots that stop a scroll. Get them in the first 3 seconds.
- Reply to every "what's the app called" comment with the name, not a link. Links tank reach.
- The money-saved number is the proof. Always show it counting.

## What converts after the view

The hard onboarding paywall is set up to convert the people the video sends. The quiz names the feeling, the projection screen shows the year ahead, then the price. Free trial on the annual plan only. The money-saved counter is the retention argument: once it passes $30, the app has paid for itself and people stop cancelling.
